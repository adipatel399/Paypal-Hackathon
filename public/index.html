HOW THIS WORKS
FIELD NOTES / 001

Agree on the conditions.
Collect when they’re met.

Participants authorize the reviewed ticket amount under the agreed terms. The organizer confirms the venue and readiness. Greenlight checks the plan before attempting collection. If settlement fails, recovery stays visible.

What this prototype can and cannot do

Simulation is enabled by default. PayPal sandbox requires credentials and verification. Local AI runs through Ollama. New text briefs are extracted into drafts that require explicit review. Greenlight is not escrow, and group capture is not atomic. Hosting, arbitrary brief extraction, and the final submission are unfinished.

A working study in conditional commerce.
